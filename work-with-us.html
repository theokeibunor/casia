How we work

Early, real work with light structure.

We're early. The work is real, the structure is light, and most days you'll have to figure something out yourself. The people who thrive here tend to:

  • Think clearly and write things down.
  • Move fast without being sloppy.
  • Talk to users, the actual shop owners, not just dashboards.
  • Take ownership of a problem end to end.
  • Improve the systems you find as you go.
