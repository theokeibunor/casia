One layer, four loops

The same architecture
under every loop.

Records, workflows, controls, and insights are shared primitives. Set once, they work everywhere the layer touches. That's what makes four tools feel like one thing.

01 · Records

Every event, a searchable record.

Stock movement, ledger entry, purchase request, customer chat. Retrievable, out of anyone's head.

02 · Workflows

One routing engine.

Approvals, hand-offs, follow-ups. Same primitives across every loop. Whoever's covering can pick up the work.

03 · Controls

Rules set once, enforced everywhere.

Reorder points, approval limits, credit terms. Governed at the loop, applied on the ground.

04 · Insights

Live signals, not reports.

Records plus rules equals real-time nudges. What's stuck, what's leaking, what needs eyes now.
