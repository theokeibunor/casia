How it works

Entered once. It moves on its own.

Here's what happens when a delivery arrives. No re-typing, no forwarding, no reconciling later.

1
Inventory

The storekeeper receives it

Scan or pick the items, confirm the quantities. Stock and item costs update straight away.

2
Casia

It goes where it's needed

Casia passes the receipt to Finance within minutes. Nobody forwards anything.

3
Finance

A person approves it

It arrives at cost, already split between stock and "received, not yet billed". Approve it, or reject it and say why.

Nothing posts to your books on its own. More tools join this flow as they connect.
